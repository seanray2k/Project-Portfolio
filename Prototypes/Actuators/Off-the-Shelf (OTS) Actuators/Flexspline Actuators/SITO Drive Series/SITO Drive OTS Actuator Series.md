# SITO Drive OTS Actuator Series

## Introduction
With ongoing research in robotics, there is a constant need for rapid prototyping of physical mechanical designs. However, this puts a higher priority on actuation modules to evaluation these concepts which consequentially consumes significants portions of allocated financial budgeting, especially if the prototypes are meant to undergo significant cyclic or fatigue testing to mechanical failure. To combat this issue, I wanted to develop a series of actuation solutions that could be used for prototype design evaluation, provide an array of output torques based on design requirements, and implement isolated, safe points of failure at the actuator level to preserve overall mechanical design with little cost to repair or replace the actuator entirely.
