# Mudskipper Quadruped Project

## Introduction
The hardware for the Mudskipper project utilizes the open-source [Barkour](https://sites.google.com/view/barkour) quadruped platform. This platform was chosen for the performance specifications across joint actuation outputs, as well as dedicated internal computing hardware (Intel NUC) optimal for implementing and testing walking/traversal policies. Majority of the body and leg structural components were optimized to be 3D printed ASA for reduced part cost and quick replacement compared to machined aluminum. Since the design was intended for metal manufacturing, several geometries and and fastened joints required additional spline supports for improved rigidity, especially across thinner profiles subject to layer-line failure.
